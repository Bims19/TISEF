Who we are
Our work centres on violence prevention and survivor support — with a focus on family harm and human trafficking — driven by lived-experience insight, community engagement, and peer-reviewed research.

A holistic model, built for the whole journey

TISEF supports vulnerable women, children, and survivors of violence through prevention initiatives, direct protection services, legal advocacy, and economic empowerment — addressing root causes like poverty, gender inequity, and lack of access to education, not just individual incidents.

  • 01 Prevention. Education and awareness that stop harm before it happens.
  • 02 Protection. Direct, trauma-informed support for survivors.
  • 03 Prosecution. Legal and justice support that holds systems accountable.
  • 04 Empowerment. Skills, income, and independence for the long term.
